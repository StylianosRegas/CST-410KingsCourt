README
Title: Kings Court
Player count: 5 - 8
Play time: 10 - 15 min
Genre: Social deduction
Hook: Kings Court is a 10 - 15 minute social deduction game for 5 - 8 players with two separate teams that aims to keep the current king in power, while traitors seek to sit on throne for themselves. Each round the king chooses an advisor to choose an action from, impacting the rest of the game. 
Editor Version: 6000.6.1f1
Branch Convention: Each member has a branch with their name attached to it. Each member will work on a different system of the game to avoid conflicts.
